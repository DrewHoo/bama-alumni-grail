The Bama Alumni Grail

One ash-gray University of Alabama Alumni crewneck — big straight-set outline ALABAMA, the university seal rising behind it, a charcoal ALUMNI ribbon below — on a made-in-USA Jerzees Super Sweats blank. It exists. One of them is being hunted.

How to read this page: the target spec is the sweatshirt in hand (photos below). Everything else is ranked by distance from it — after passing two hard gates: it has to be gray, and it has to be an L or an XL. Crimson versions of the print are kept below as comps, never as options; XXLs are not kept at all. Prices & availability as of Oct 26, 2026 — vintage listings die fast; buy buttons go to the live listing. The target

Photographed in hand. The neck tag is Jerzees Super Sweats, 42-44 (a 90s Large), Made in U.S.A.; the care tag is Russell Corp, Alexander City, AL — WPL 7332, ~50/50 poly-cotton with 5% other fiber. That tag combo dates it to roughly 1988–1995.

Match checklist (in priority order)

Color hard gate
Must be a shade of gray. Ash and light heather are ideal, charcoal is viable. Everything else is an automatic pass no matter how right the print is — red/crimson, white, cream, oatmeal, sand and black are out. Checked before the graphic.
Graphic
Straight-set serif ALABAMA — charcoal-heather fill, crimson outline; circular University of Alabama seal rising behind the letters; charcoal ALUMNI ribbon banner below
Blank
Jerzees Super Sweats (Russell Corp), Made in USA — same-era Russell/Jerzees acceptable
Size hard gate
L or XL only. 42-44 (a 90s L) is ideal and a modern L–XL is fine. XXL is out — it wears too big, and no print is good enough to buy around it.
Condition
Honest wear fine; no large stains or holes
Price guide
Comps say $20–$50; up to ~$75 for a true exact match is rational grail money
