The target

Photographed in hand. The neck tag is Jerzees Super Sweats, 42-44 (a 90s Large), Made in U.S.A.; the care tag is Russell Corp, Alexander City, AL — WPL 7332, ~50/50 poly-cotton with 5% other fiber. That tag combo dates it to roughly 1988–1995.

Match checklist (in priority order)

Color hard gate
Must be a shade of gray. Ash and light heather are ideal, charcoal is viable. Everything else is an automatic pass no matter how right the print is — red/crimson, white, cream, oatmeal, sand and black are out. Checked before the graphic.
Graphic
Straight-set serif ALABAMA — charcoal-heather fill, crimson outline; circular University of Alabama seal rising behind the letters; charcoal ALUMNI ribbon banner below
Blank
Jerzees Super Sweats (Russell Corp), Made in USA — same-era Russell/Jerzees acceptable
Size
42-44 / L (fits like a modern L–XL); XL acceptable
Condition
Honest wear fine; no large stains or holes
Price guide
Comps say $20–$50; up to ~$75 for a true exact match is rational grail money
